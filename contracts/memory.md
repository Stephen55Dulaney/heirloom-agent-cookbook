## What this capability is (the seed)

Memory is how the companion knows *this specific person*. Without it, every conversation
starts from zero and the companion becomes a stranger with every session.

A good memory contract should specify, in plain language:

- **What gets stored** — observations, not raw transcripts.
- **What gets promoted to long-term** — durable patterns, not passing moments.
- **What the person controls** — everything; consent before capture, always (Care Doctrine
  Rule 3).
- **How memory survives a reboot** — it must outlive a power cycle.
- **How it's recalled naturally** — *"You told me your favorite color is blue,"* never
  *"According to my memory log entry, you stated…"*

## What it must never do

- Capture, log, or share anything without consent.
- Dredge up things the person hasn't brought up themselves.
- Lose what the person trusted it to hold.

Pick a primary metric, write the acceptance criteria, capture the scars, and submit it.
