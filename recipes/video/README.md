# The Edit Bay: the video recipes

> A recipe is a promise that someone else can make the dish. A video is how most people find out the
> dish exists. This wing turns the work you already did into something people can watch, without
> anyone pretending to be you.

This wing holds **four recipe cards for a small, honest video pipeline**: from a topic, to a filmed
take, to a finished upload waiting for a human to press Publish. Like every card in this cookbook, they
contain **no code**. Each one says what the stage must do, how you know it worked, the lines it must
never cross, and the scars from when it broke. Hand a card to Claude Code, Codex, or any capable agent
and say *"build this stage for my channel."*

They were written from a pipeline that runs on one person's laptop and publishes a real channel. Every
scar below was paid for in production.

## The line that runs through all four

**The person narrates. The machine gets out of the way.** The machine cuts, times, captions, builds the
visuals, checks for private details, and prepares the upload. It never writes words and puts them in the
narrator's mouth, never synthesizes their voice, and never presses Publish. A video of someone explaining
their own work is worth making *because* it's them.

## The stations, in the order you cook them

| # | Card | Takes | Gives back |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **[The package before you film](package-before-you-film.md)** | a topic, a link, or a finished piece of work | a research brief, hooks, a script in your voice, per-platform copy, a shot list |
| 2 | **[A walkthrough from a phone video](walkthrough-from-a-phone-video.md)** | 5–8 minutes of you explaining your own system | a cut, slides built from your own words, a corner-box composite, chapters, a thumbnail |
| 3 | **[Motion on your words](motion-on-your-words.md)** | a cut video and a word-timed transcript | animated B-roll whose every change lands on a spoken word |
| 4 | **[A Short from a raw clip](short-from-a-raw-clip.md)** | one raw vertical take | a graded, captioned, privacy-checked bundle, uploaded as a **private** draft |

Station 3 is a technique you can add to 2 or 4. The rest stand alone.

## Three gates before anything is public

1. **A privacy pass.** Read the transcript *and look at the frames* for any name, place, health detail,
   address, key, or face of someone who didn't agree to be in it. A family's city is private even when
   the family isn't named.
2. **No comparisons with a former employer**, named or not. The team recognizes itself.
3. **Only claims you can show.** If a number is on screen, it came from a measurement you kept.
