# Recipe: The Package Before You Film

### One topic in, one folder out, and you walk to the camera with it in your hand

For a long time our pipeline started at a raw clip: everything clever happened *after* filming. This
recipe is the upstream half. You bring a topic (an idea, a link, a thing you just finished building);
you get back everything you need to film it well and post it everywhere, before the camera is on.

---

## What you're building

A folder, one per video, containing:

- **A research brief.** What's true about the topic, with every fact pointing at its source. If the topic
  is your own work, the source is your own records: the logbook, the ticket, the commit.
- **Hooks and titles.** Several, so you can choose.
- **A script in your voice**, timed in beats, for you to speak. Your edits to it are kept.
- **Per-platform copy** (the video description, a post, an essay intro), each shaped for where it goes.
- **A thumbnail brief and a shot list**: what's on screen, taken from your real screen, desk, and work.
- **A run record**: what was asked, what was made, and later what you changed and how it performed.

---

## The contract

Given a topic, the package MUST:

1. **Read your voice first.** A short document of how you actually talk: phrases you use, ones you'd never
   use, the rhythm of your sentences. It's built up from your own edits.
2. **Keep every story true and built from your own material.** No staged failures, and no borrowed frames
   from other creators' videos used as visuals.
3. **Credit other people's work only as far as the evidence shows**: a name on screen, a handle in frame.
   Never guess who made something.
4. **Keep every fact traceable** to where it came from.
5. **Post nothing.** The package drafts; the person posts. Video goes through the Short or walkthrough
   stations as a private draft.
6. **Close the loop.** After you film and edit, record every change you made to the script or copy,
   word for word. Those edits are how the next package sounds more like you.

---

## How you know it worked

- [ ] **You'd say the script out loud without rewriting half of it.** Count your edits; the count should
      shrink over time.
- [ ] **Every claim has a source** you could click.
- [ ] **Nothing was posted** by the package itself.
- [ ] **The shot list uses your real material**, with nothing borrowed.

| Measure | Minimum | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Your edits to the script, per video | falling over time | a handful |
| Facts without a source | 0 | 0 |

---

## Known hard parts (the scars)

**1. The machine's script was worse than the person's rambling.** On the walkthrough that started this
wing, the person ignored the script and said something better. A package is a starting point, not lines
to read. For explaining your own system, film the walkthrough instead and build from what you say.

**2. Age as a headline.** "At my age I…" is a tool inside a story, never the story's title.

**3. Voice drifts unless your edits are kept.** Guessing someone's voice doesn't improve it. Recording the
exact changes they make does.
